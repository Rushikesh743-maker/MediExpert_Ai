# Clinical Knowledge Base References & Evidence Sources

This document catalogs the authoritative medical and public health references used to structure the 50 IF–THEN production rules in **MediExpert AI**. 

All rules are constructed using clinical symptom constellation guidelines published by major public health institutes, clinical societies, and national healthcare systems.

## 3. Scope and Ethical Boundaries

These references serve as structural templates for codifying propositional Boolean rules in an Artificial Intelligence expert system architecture. They do not constitute certified medical advice, clinical diagnosis protocols, or pharmaceutical prescriptions.
